The Minimal Mono look in one line

Black, white and warm grey. Square frames, grayscale photos, a heavy sans-serif title and black text cards.

What to put on it

  • A study desk for exams and grad school
  • A laptop office for career
  • A small business packing station for your brand
  • Running shoes for health
  • Books for growth

In Glowboard you just tap the goals you want (travel, money, love, style and more) and the matching photos and affirmations are added for you. You can also add your own photos. They stay on your phone.

Tips for a board you will actually look at

  1. Use numbers: “$10k saved”, “4.0 GPA”, “100 customers”.
  2. Fewer words per card. Bold sans-serif reads best at a glance.
  3. Pair with a Bold title style for maximum contrast.
Affirmations that fit this look
  • “Discipline is self-love”
  • “Straight A energy”
  • “My business is booming”
  • “Focused, calm, prepared”
  • “I keep my promises to myself”

How to set it as your lock screen

iPhone: tap Save image (or long-press and choose “Save to Photos”). Open Photos, tap Share, then Use as Wallpaper. Android: open the image in Gallery or Google Photos, tap ⋮, then Set as wallpaper, then Lock screen.
