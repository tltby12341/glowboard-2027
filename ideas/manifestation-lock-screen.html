Why your lock screen is the best place to manifest

Manifestation practices all share one idea: keep your attention on what you want. You unlock your phone around 100 times a day. Putting your intentions there means you see them on autopilot, not just when you remember to open a journal.

How to build a manifestation lock screen

  1. Choose 3–5 intentions for 2027, such as money, love, career or peace.
  2. Write them in the present tense, as if they are already true: “I am financially free”, not “I want money”.
  3. Add a photo of the feeling, not the effort: the view from the trip, not the plane ticket.
  4. Pick one word of the year as your anchor: abundance, softness, devotion.
  5. Keep the top clear so the clock does not cover your words. Glowboard does this automatically.
Try the 3-6-9 with your lock screen: each time you see your board in the morning, afternoon and evening, read one affirmation out loud. It takes 10 seconds.

FAQ

Do manifestation wallpapers work?

A wallpaper keeps your goals in front of you all day, which makes it easier to stay focused and act on them. Pair it with one small action per goal.

What should I write on a manifestation lock screen?

Short present-tense affirmations (“I am financially free”), one word of the year, and photos of the outcome you want.
