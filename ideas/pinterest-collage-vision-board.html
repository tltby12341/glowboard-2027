2027 AESTHETIC · MOOD COLLAGE

A Pinterest-style collage vision board for your phone

The messy-on-purpose collage you see all over Pinterest: taped photos, sticky notes, stickers and graph paper. Mood Collage makes your goals feel like a real, handmade board.

Make a Mood Collage board with my goals →

The Mood Collage look in one line

Graph-paper cream with washi tape, butter-yellow sticky notes, tomato red and blue stickers, and a handwritten title.

What to put on it

  • Travel spots you have pinned all year
  • An art studio for creative goals
  • Friends and celebrations
  • A cozy date night for love
  • Outfits for your style era

In Glowboard you just tap the goals you want (travel, money, love, style and more) and the matching photos and affirmations are added for you. You can also add your own photos. They stay on your phone.

Tips for a board you will actually look at

  1. Write goals like quick notes: “Learn to film photos”.
  2. Shuffle a few times. The tilts and tape change each time.
  3. Upload a few of your own photos for a real scrapbook feel.
Affirmations that fit this look
  • “Make it messy, make it mine”
  • “My sketchbook is full”
  • “Adventure finds me easily”
  • “More laughter, more memories”
  • “Signature style era”

How to set it as your lock screen

iPhone: tap Save image (or long-press and choose “Save to Photos”). Open Photos, tap Share, then Use as Wallpaper. Android: open the image in Gallery or Google Photos, tap ⋮, then Set as wallpaper, then Lock screen.

FAQ

Yes. Tap any photo in the preview to swap it.
