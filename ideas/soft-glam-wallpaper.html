The Soft Glam look in one line

Rose, blush, champagne and pearl white. Glowing rounded frames, scattered pearls, an italic serif title and a script word of the year.

What to put on it

  • A pink vanity with brushes and perfume for beauty goals
  • A bubble bath for self-care
  • Peonies or roses for love
  • A silk blouse and gold jewelry for style
  • A soft, sunlit bedroom for your dream home
