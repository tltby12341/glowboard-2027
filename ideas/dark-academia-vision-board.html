The Dark Academia look in one line

Espresso brown, parchment, burgundy and antique gold. Matted cream frames, lined note cards and an italic serif title.

What to put on it

  • A grand library for grad school or exams
  • A candle-lit desk with a fountain pen for writing goals
  • An art studio for creative projects
  • A meditation corner for calm focus
  • A European city for a study-abroad dream

In Glowboard you just tap the goals you want (travel, money, love, style and more) and the matching photos and affirmations are added for you. You can also add your own photos. They stay on your phone.

Tips for a board you will actually look at

  1. Write study goals with a date: “Pass the bar in July”.
  2. Use the Serif or Handwritten title style for an old-letter feel.
  3. Pair it with the printable goals checklist for your desk.
Affirmations that fit this look
  • “I graduate in 2027”
  • “One page at a time”
  • “Discipline beats motivation”
  • “I love learning”
  • “Creativity flows through me”

How to set it as your lock screen

iPhone: tap Save image (or long-press and choose “Save to Photos”). Open Photos, tap Share, then Use as Wallpaper. Android: open the image in Gallery or Google Photos, tap ⋮, then Set as wallpaper, then Lock screen.
