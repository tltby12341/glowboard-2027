The Old Money Winter look in one line

Snow white, cream and camel with deep navy text cards and antique gold lines. Light snowfall in the background.

What to put on it

  • A ski-chalet terrace for a winter trip
  • A snowy cabin for a getaway
  • A capsule wardrobe in cream and camel
  • A fireplace with cocoa for cozy goals
  • A leather planner for money goals

In Glowboard you just tap the goals you want (travel, money, love, style and more) and the matching photos and affirmations are added for you. You can also add your own photos. They stay on your phone.

Tips for a board you will actually look at

  1. Navy cards with cream text look sharp on a light lock screen.
  2. Keep it to one trip goal and one money goal.
  3. Switch to Old Money (green) in spring for a matching set.
Affirmations that fit this look
  • “Quality over quantity”

  • “Rich in every way”

  • “I invest in timeless pieces”

How to set it as your lock screen

iPhone: tap Save image (or long-press and choose “Save to Photos”). Open Photos, tap Share, then Use as Wallpaper. Android: open the image in Gallery or Google Photos, tap ⋮, then Set as wallpaper, then Lock screen.
