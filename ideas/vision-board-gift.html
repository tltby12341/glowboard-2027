A vision board gift for 2027: personal, instant, no shipping

Looking for a thoughtful last-minute gift? Make your friend a 2027 vision board for her lock screen, or gift her Glowboard Pro so she can make her own.

Why a vision board makes a great gift

It is personal, it costs little or nothing, and unlike a candle it gets used every day. Everyone is thinking about the new year in December, so a board made for them lands at exactly the right moment.

Option 1: Design one for them (free)

  1. Open the maker and pick their goals: the trip they keep mentioning, the business they want to start.
  2. Choose the aesthetic they would love (coquette for the romantic, old money for the classic one, mob wife for the bold one).
  3. Set the title to something personal: “Your 2027, Em” or “Her Era”.
  4. Tap Send this board to a friend. They open the link, see the board you made for them, and can save it as their wallpaper.

Option 2: Gift Glowboard Pro ($4.99)

Gumroad checkout has a “Give as a gift?” option. Tick it and enter their email, and they receive Pro: all 16 looks, no watermark, a matching home screen, iPad and desktop sizes, and a printable goals checklist.

Who it is perfect for

  • Your best friend before New Year’s Eve
  • Your sister or mom as a stocking stuffer
  • A long-distance friend (no shipping)
  • Coworkers for a team “2027 goals” moment
  • Your partner, with a board of trips you will take together

Host a vision board party

Invite friends over, put on a playlist, and have everyone make their board on their phone at the same time. Share your template links in the group chat so people can remix each other’s boards.

What to write with your gift

  • “Made you something for 2027 ✨”
  • “This is your year. Here is proof.”
  • “Saw this and thought of your goals 💌”
  • “Lock screen, but make it your future.”
