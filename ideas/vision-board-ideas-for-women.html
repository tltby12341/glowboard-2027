FAQ

What should a woman put on her vision board?

Choose 4–7 life areas (for example career, money, health, love, self-care, home, travel) and add one specific goal and one photo for each, plus a word of the year.

How many pictures should a vision board have?

On a phone, 6–8 photos plus 2–3 short affirmations is the sweet spot. Any more becomes noise on a small screen.
